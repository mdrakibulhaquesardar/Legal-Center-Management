# User Data & Account Deletion Policy

**Application Name:** Habit Forge - Daily Routine  
**Package Name:** `com.nexcode.habitforge`  
**Developer:** NexCode Studio  
**Compliance Reference:** Google Play User Data & Account Deletion Policy  

At Habit Forge by **NexCode Studio**, we respect your privacy and provide transparent, straightforward mechanisms for users to request the permanent deletion of their account and associated personal data.

---

## 1. What Data Can Be Deleted?

When you initiate a deletion request, the following records are permanently purged from our active systems:
* **User Profile Data:** Your registered email address, name, profile picture, and user ID.
* **Habit & Activity Records:** All custom habits, streaks, schedules, progress logs, completion checkmarks, and notes.
* **Device Identifiers & Push Tokens:** Registered FCM notification tokens associated with your account.
* **Cloud Backup Files:** Encrypted synchronization snapshots stored in cloud databases.

---

## 2. Option A: In-App Account Deletion (Instant)

You can delete your account and all associated data directly from within the mobile app:

1. Open the **Habit Forge** app on your Android device.
2. Tap the **Settings** icon located in the top navigation bar.
3. Scroll down to the **Account & Security** section.
4. Tap **Delete Account**.
5. Read the confirmation warning and tap **Permanently Delete My Account**.
6. Upon confirmation, your cloud data is immediately deleted and your current session is logged out.

---

## 3. Option B: Web Request via Support Email (Alternative Deletion Method)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, you can request account deletion via email:

1. Send an email to **[support@nexcode.studio](mailto:support@nexcode.studio)** with the subject line:  
   `Account Deletion Request - Habit Forge`
2. In the body of the message, provide:
   * The registered email address linked to your Habit Forge account.
   * A brief statement confirming you want all associated data erased.
3. To protect account integrity, our support team may send a verification email to your registered address to confirm ownership.
4. Once verified, all your data will be permanently wiped within **7 business days**. You will receive a final confirmation email upon completion.

---

## 4. Data Retention Exceptions

Certain anonymized telemetry data (such as non-identifiable crash counts and aggregate app usage trends) cannot be linked back to individual users and may be retained for general performance metrics. Any data required by applicable legal, tax, or regulatory compliance mandates will be held strictly for the legally required duration and deleted thereafter.
