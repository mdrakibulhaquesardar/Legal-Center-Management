# Habit Forge Support & Help Center

Welcome to the official support page for **Habit Forge - Daily Routine** by **NexCode Studio**. We are committed to providing you with a seamless habit-building experience.

---

## 1. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I restore my purchases?
If you have upgraded to Habit Forge Pro and reinstalled the app or switched devices:
1. Open **Settings** in the app.
2. Tap **Restore Purchases**.
3. Ensure you are signed into the Google Play Store with the same Google account used for the original purchase.

### Why didn't I receive my scheduled reminder?
On modern Android versions, aggressive battery optimization settings can suppress background alarms:
* Go to your phone's **Settings > Apps > Habit Forge > Battery** and select **Unrestricted**.
* Ensure the **POST_NOTIFICATIONS** permission is granted under the app permission settings.

### How does cloud sync work?
Cloud sync runs automatically whenever your device connects to the internet. Your habits, completions, and notes sync seamlessly across all Android devices where you are logged into your account.

## 2. Report a Bug or Request a Feature

We actively listen to user feedback to improve Habit Forge. If you encounter an unexpected crash, visual glitch, or have an idea for a new feature:

* **Email:** [support@nexcode.studio](mailto:support@nexcode.studio)
* **Response Time:** We typically respond within 24–48 hours on business days.
* Please include your **Android OS version**, **device model**, and a description of the issue.
