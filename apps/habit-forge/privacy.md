# Privacy Policy for Habit Forge

**Effective Date:** November 8, 2026  
**Application Name:** Habit Forge - Daily Routine  
**Package Name:** `com.devhridoy.habitforge`  
**Developer Contact:** support@habitforge.app  

This Privacy Policy describes how Habit Forge ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our mobile application available on the Google Play Store.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide Directly
* **Account Information:** When you voluntarily register with email, Google Sign-In, or Apple ID, we collect your email address and profile name.
* **Habit & Routine Data:** Names of habits, task schedules, completion timestamps, streaks, and notes you create within the app.
* **Customer Support Inquiries:** Information and email address provided when contacting our support desk.

### B. Information Collected Automatically
* **Device Information:** Device model, operating system version (Android), unique device identifiers, and language settings.
* **Diagnostics & Crash Logs:** Anonymized crash logs and app performance statistics collected via Firebase Crashlytics to resolve bugs and enhance stability.
* **Usage Statistics:** Aggregate metrics about feature usage, session duration, and retention patterns.

---

## 2. Google Play Data Safety Compliance

In compliance with Google Play Data Safety specifications:
* **Personal Data:** Collected only with user consent for authentication and account sync.
* **Data Transmission:** All data exchanged between the app and cloud servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard **TLS / HTTPS**.
* **Third-Party Data Sharing:** We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared exclusively with necessary infrastructure providers (such as Google Cloud / Firebase) solely to operate the service.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We process collected data for the following lawful purposes:
1. To deliver core app functionality (tracking habits, syncing across devices, calculating streaks).
2. To send local reminder notifications scheduled by you on your device.
3. To monitor, diagnose, and fix software defects and optimize application performance.
4. To respond to customer inquiries and fulfill support requests.

---

## 4. Permissions Requested

* **POST_NOTIFICATIONS:** To deliver habit reminders and motivational alarms that you have configured.
* **SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM:** To ensure habit reminder notifications trigger at the precise scheduled minute.
* **INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE:** Required for cloud synchronization and checking connectivity status.

---

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain personal data only for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide our services. You retain full control over your data:
* You may delete individual habits and records directly inside the app.
* You may request permanent deletion of your complete account and all associated cloud data at any time.
* For comprehensive deletion procedures, please review our official [Data Deletion Policy](/habit-forge/data-deletion).

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Habit Forge is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can promptly purge the data.

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy. Any modifications will be posted directly to this permanent URL with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
