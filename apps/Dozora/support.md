# Dozora Support & Help Center

Welcome to the official support desk and user guide for **Dozora: Better Sleep**, developed by **NexCode Studio**.

Dozora is crafted to help you quiet a racing mind, reduce stress, practice mindfulness, and enjoy a restorative night's sleep using high-definition ambient soundscapes, white noise, and gentle sleep timers.

---

## 1. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is Dozora really 100% free with no ads?
**Yes!** Dozora is completely free to download and use. There are **zero commercial advertisements** (no AdMob banners, no interstitial pop-ups, and no video ads) and **zero in-app purchases or subscriptions**. You have unlimited access to all ambient sounds, timers, and mixing features without paying anything.

### Does audio continue playing when my phone screen turns off?
**Yes!** Dozora runs as an Android Foreground Media Service (`FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK`). You can lock your phone, turn off the screen, or switch to other applications while your soothing soundscapes continue playing smoothly in the background.

### Can I blend and mix multiple sounds together?
**Yes!** With Dozora's multi-track Sound Mixer, you can layer different natural elements (for example, *Rain on Window* + *Distant Thunder* + *Campfire*) and adjust individual volume sliders for each track to build your perfect sleep sanctuary. You can also save your favorite customized blends for quick one-tap access later.

### Does the app work completely offline?
**Yes!** All soundscapes, nature recordings, and relaxation algorithms are bundled locally into the application. Dozora works 100% offline—perfect for flights, remote cabins, or when you switch your phone to Airplane Mode for uninterrupted sleep.

### How does the Sleep Timer work?
The Sleep Timer allows you to drift off without worrying about your battery draining all night. Tap the Timer icon, select your desired duration (e.g., 15 mins, 30 mins, 1 hour, or custom), and the app will gently fade out the audio when time expires.

### How do I prevent Android from killing background playback?
Some Android manufacturers (e.g., Xiaomi MIUI, Samsung One UI, OnePlus OxygenOS, Huawei) use aggressive battery savers that can prematurely terminate background audio apps. To prevent this:
1. Open your phone's **Settings** &rarr; **Apps** &rarr; **Dozora**.
2. Tap **Battery** (or **App Battery Usage**).
3. Select **"Unrestricted"** (or disable "Battery Optimization" for Dozora).

### Is Dozora a substitute for medical sleep treatments?
**No.** Dozora is an audio relaxation and general wellness tool. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, or cure clinical sleep disorders such as chronic insomnia or sleep apnea. If you suffer from medical sleep issues, please consult a qualified healthcare provider.
