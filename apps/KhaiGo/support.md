## 2. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is KhaiGO really 100% free with no ads?
**Yes!** KhaiGO is a passion project and community culinary guide created by NexCode Studio. There are **no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no payment gateways, and zero advertisements** (no Google AdMob or banner ads). You can explore all dishes and features without paying a single cent.

### Which areas are currently supported?
KhaiGO focuses on **Rajshahi, Bangladesh**, covering major dining zones including:
* Shaheb Bazar & Zero Point
* Kazla, Motihar, and RUET campus area
* Rajshahi University (RU) and station area
* Laxmipur & Medical College area
* Padma Garden & Riverfront culinary stalls
* Rajpara, Alupatti, and Sagarpara

### What is "Chef Panda's Surprise Me"?
When you and your friends can't decide what to eat, tap **Chef Panda's Surprise Me**! The smart culinary algorithm randomly spins and picks a curated local dish based on current time of day (breakfast, lunch, evening snacks, or dinner) and your proximity.

### Does KhaiGO deliver food to my doorstep?
**No.** KhaiGO is a food and restaurant discovery guide, not a food delivery company. We do not have delivery riders or online order processing. We help you find the best dishes, check estimated prices, view menu photos, and get directions so you can visit or contact the restaurant directly.

### Do I need to share my GPS location to use KhaiGO?
No! Geolocation is completely optional. If you grant location permission, KhaiGO sorts dishes by closest distance to you in real-time. If you deny permission, you can easily pick your neighborhood manually from the top selector.

### How do I submit or edit a food review?
Log in to your profile, search for the dish you enjoyed, and tap **"Write a Review"**. You can give a 1-5 star rating, write a detailed culinary review, and upload your food photos.

### How do I delete my account?
You can delete your account instantly from within the app:
* Navigate to **Profile** &rarr; **Settings** &rarr; **Account Management** &rarr; **Delete Account**.
* Alternatively, email [rakibul.nexcode@gmail.com](mailto:rakibul.nexcode@gmail.com) with the subject `KhaiGO Account Deletion Request`.
