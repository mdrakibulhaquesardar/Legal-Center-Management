# KhaiGO Data & Account Deletion Policy

In full compliance with **Google Play's Account Deletion Policy**, **NexCode Studio** guarantees that users of **KhaiGO** have complete, unhindered control over their personal data and accounts.

You may request the complete deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time through our **direct in-app interface** or via **remote email request**.

---

## 1. How to Delete Your Account In-App (Instant)

If you have the KhaiGO application installed on your Android device, you can permanently delete your account immediately without contacting support:

1. Launch the **KhaiGO** app on your phone.
2. Tap the **Profile** icon in the bottom navigation bar.
3. Tap the **Settings (gear)** icon located at the top-right corner of the profile screen.
4. Scroll down to the **Account Management** section.
5. Tap **"Delete Account"**.
6. Review the confirmation dialog and tap **"Confirm Delete"**.

> [!NOTE]
> Upon confirmation, your session is immediately terminated, your authentication credentials in Supabase are invalidated, and your profile details are irreversibly removed from our active database.

---

## 3. Scope of Data Deletion

When an account deletion request is processed:

### Data That Is Permanently Erased:
* **Account Credentials:** Email address, hashed password, phone number, and unique Supabase User ID (`UUID`).
* **Profile Metadata:** Display name, bio, profile photo/avatar URL, and culinary preferences.
* **Bookmarked Items:** All saved foods, favorite dining spots, and custom bookmark lists.
* **Notification Tokens:** Device-specific Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push notification tokens.
* **User Submissions:** Personal dish ratings, reviews, and uploaded food photos submitted under your profile.

### Data That May Be Temporarily Retained:
* **Aggregated Crash Metrics:** Anonymized, non-personally identifiable diagnostic stack traces collected by Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose system crashes. These metrics do not contain personal identifiers and automatically expire under standard Google Firebase retention schedules.
* **Legal Compliance Records:** Minimal audit logs indicating that a deletion request was executed, retained strictly for fraud prevention and regulatory compliance.
