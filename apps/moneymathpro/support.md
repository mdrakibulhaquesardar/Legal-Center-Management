# MoneyMath Pro Support & Help Center

Welcome to the official support and documentation desk for **MoneyMath Pro**, developed by **NexCode Studio**.

## 1. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is MoneyMath Pro really 100% offline?
**Yes!** MoneyMath Pro executes all financial algorithms natively on your phone CPU. It operates completely offline without needing mobile data, Wi-Fi, or an active cloud connection.

### Is my personal financial data safe on my phone?
**100% safe.** MoneyMath Pro does not collect, record, or upload any user inputs or calculation history to external servers. All data stays strictly in your phone's local memory.

### Do I need to create an account or log in?
No registration, email login, or phone number verification is required. You can install and start using all 32+ calculators immediately.

### How does the Loan EMI calculator work?
It uses the standard reducing balance interest method utilized by Bangladeshi banks (DBBL, City Bank, BRAC Bank, etc.). It calculates monthly EMI, total interest, and total payable amount based on loan principal, annual interest rate, and tenure.

### Are Income Tax calculations updated with NBR 2025–2026 guidelines?
**Yes!** The tax engine incorporates the latest National Board of Revenue (NBR) Bangladesh tax assessment rules, including general tax-free allowances (৳3,50,000 for general taxpayers, higher for female/senior citizens) and progressive slab rates.

### How is Zakat calculated?
According to Islamic principles, Zakat is mandatory at 2.5% if your total gold ownership reaches or exceeds the Nisab threshold of 7.5 Bhori (87.48 grams). The calculator converts your bhori or gram weight to current market value and displays exact Zakat due.

### Does the app include bKash, Nagad, and Rocket cashout fee calculators?
**Yes!** You can calculate exact cashout charges for App vs USSD (`*247#` / `*167#`) transactions for bKash, Nagad, and Rocket, helping users and merchants know exact net amounts.
