# User Data & Account Deletion Policy

**Application Name:** MoneyMath Pro  
**Package Name:** `com.nexcode.studio.moneymath.pro`  
**Developer:** NexCode Studio  
**Compliance Reference:** Google Play User Data & Account Deletion Policy  

MoneyMath Pro by **NexCode Studio** is architected as an offline-first calculator. Because the app does not require account registration or cloud database storage for user calculations, your personal financial data remains strictly local to your device.

---

## 1. How to Delete Your Local Data Instantly

All calculation histories, saved estimates, and custom loan inputs are stored exclusively on your device's internal storage:
1. Open your Android device's **Settings**.
2. Navigate to **Apps > MoneyMath Pro > Storage**.
3. Tap **Clear Data** (or **Clear Storage**).
4. All calculation history, cached values, and saved preferences will be erased immediately.
5. Alternatively, uninstalling the app permanently purges all local app data from your phone.
