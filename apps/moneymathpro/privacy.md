## 4. 100% Local Financial Calculations (Zero Data Upload)

MoneyMath Pro executes all financial algorithms natively on your phone CPU. The app operates completely offline without needing mobile data or a cloud connection:
* Your custom loan amounts, interest rates, salary figures, and Zakat inputs are **never uploaded** to any server.
* All calculations remain strictly stored in your device's private local memory.
