# Privacy Policy for Audio Flow Recorder

**Effective Date:** November 8, 2026  
**Application Name:** Audio Flow Recorder  
**Package Name:** `com.nexcode.audioflow`  
**Developer Contact:** support@nexcode.studio  

Audio Flow Recorder by **NexCode Studio** ("we", "our") is designed with a strict **privacy-first architecture**. We firmly believe that your voice recordings and audio files belong solely to you.

---

## 1. Zero Audio Upload Policy

* **100% Local Processing:** All sound recordings captured through Audio Flow Recorder are processed and stored exclusively on your device's internal memory or private storage directory.
* **No Cloud Transmission:** We do **not** upload, stream, analyze, or transmit your voice notes or audio recordings to any external server or third-party service.
* **No User Accounts:** You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide any email address to use Audio Flow Recorder.

---

## 2. Device Permissions Required

* **RECORD_AUDIO:** Required strictly to access your microphone and create audio recordings initiated by you.
* **READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE:** Required solely to save and export recorded sound files to your device's storage directory.

---

## 3. Analytics & Diagnostics

To maintain software reliability and prevent crashes, the application may collect anonymous diagnostic telemetries (such as operating system version, device model, and crash dumps) via Google Play Core and Firebase Crashlytics. No audio, filenames, or personal identifiers are ever included in diagnostic packets.
